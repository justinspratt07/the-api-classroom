High-contrast schemes, fonts, and sizes apply throughout the course. Built-in read aloud uses a local English voice on your device. Audio never starts automatically.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and arrow keys for menus and quiz choices. You can also use a full screen reader such as NVDA, VoiceOver, or Narrator.

    About, privacy, and accessibility

    Based on Justin Spratt’s grading microservice. Practice simulates the source rules; no server is contacted and no real student data is used.

    Standalone progress and preferences stay in this browser when storage is available. In an LMS launch, score, completion, and resume state are sent to that LMS; reading preferences remain local. Built-in speech uses only device-local English voices. Voice availability varies by browser and operating system. Screen-reader support does not depend on built-in speech.

    There are no timers, animations, drag-only tasks, or required audio. One task appears at a time. Enlarged text may scroll vertically to preserve access.
